Have Questions About Solar?

Find answers to some common questions about our renewable-energy products, solutions and services.

What does GARG GREEN ENERGY provide?

GARG GREEN ENERGY provides renewable-energy products, electrical components, installation, commissioning and service support.

What types of customers do you serve?

Our solutions are suitable for homes, shops, offices, factories, schools and institutions, farmers and government project requirements.

What products are available?

Our current product range includes inverters, ACDB, DCDB, AC/DC cables, GI structures, earthing, light arresters and other related products. Solar panels are currently coming soon.

Are solar panels currently available?

Solar panels are currently not available for sale through GARG GREEN ENERGY. This product category is coming soon.

Do you provide installation and commissioning?

Yes. Installation and commissioning are among the services provided by GARG GREEN ENERGY.

Do you provide service and maintenance?

Yes. Service and maintenance support is available, including preventive on-site service and applicable breakdown-call support during the warranty period.

Do you provide remote connectivity and Wi-Fi support?

Yes. Remote connectivity and Wi-Fi support are provided for supported solar systems.

You can use our Contact page and submit the enquiry form with your requirement and contact details.

Our head office is in Sanatan Nagar, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. We also have a location at Sehda Mahrajpur, Azamgarh.
